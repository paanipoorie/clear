Crowdsourced Local Environmental Action & Resolution

C.L.E.A.R. bridges the communication gap between citizens and municipal authorities, empowering communities to report, track, and resolve local environmental issues collaboratively.

How C.L.E.A.R. Works

A transparent, closed-loop resolution pipeline from report to resolution.

1

1. Report Evidence

Citizens pinpoint issues on an interactive map, upload photo evidence, and attach supporting references.

2

2. Triage Board

Municipal Operations Officers triage reports, assign squads, and track progress transparently on a board.

3

3. Action & Verify

Field crews resolve the environmental hazard. Officers upload "After" photos, closing the public loop.

Key Capabilities

Engineered for real municipal operations and active civic participation.

Role-Aware Workspaces

Dedicated dashboard configurations scoped for Residents, District Authorities, and Investigators.

Multi-Contributor Evidence

Attach or link supporting photos, maps, and external URLs to expand documentation on active reports.

Interactive GIS Mapping

Drop precise coordinates utilizing Leaflet.js and OpenStreetMap to pinpoint issues in local districts.

Official Notice Bulletins

Municipal officers publish district-wide environmental bulletins, sorting drives, and air quality notices.

Print-to-PDF Report Export

Download structured report cards containing coordinates, timeline, resolution logs, and before/after comparisons.

Real-Time Alerts

Get notification feed updates automatically when followed issue statuses change or new notices are published.

Our Mission

Restoring Trust & Accountability in Civic Infrastructure

Traditional municipal reporting channels are slow, non-transparent, and lack spatial precision. This leads to delayed responses and a lack of public visibility into whether and how reported issues are resolved.

C.L.E.A.R. fosters clean, safe, and sustainable neighborhoods by connecting citizens and authorities in a direct, digital dialog. By acting as the eyes on the ground, residents help squads optimize dispatch routes, track resolution history, and keep our natural local environments thriving.
